INTERPRETATION / 02

Why cooperation fails.

In a single round, defecting provides a greater payoff regardless of what the other player chooses. Yet if both defect, they each earn only one point, rather than three points through mutual cooperation.

Repeated games introduce history and the possibility of reciprocity. Try playing several rounds against Tit for Tat, then compare the results with an opponent that always defects.

This simulation uses illustrative payoff values and deterministic computer strategies. It is not a prediction of how real people will behave.
