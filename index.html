THE METHOD / 001

Behind the model.

Every simulation depends on assumptions. This experiment uses linear supply and demand, a single homogeneous good, and a competitive market. Its results illustrate economic theory rather than forecast the behavior of a real economy.

Demand P = a − 0.8Q
Supply P = c + 0.8Q
Tax-adjusted quantity Q = max(0, (a − c − t) / 1.6)
Deadweight loss DWL = 0.8(Q₀ − Q)²

ABOUT ECONLAB / 001

Understanding the economy, one experiment at a time.

Our Mission

EconLab started with a simple goal: to better understand how the economy works through data and experimentation.

I wanted to create a place where I could explore economic ideas beyond what I learn in a classroom, while making those same concepts easier for others to understand.

By combining economics, data, and interactive simulations, EconLab allows anyone to ask questions, test different scenarios, and see how economic decisions shape the world around us.
